Privacy Policy

The SOTA design system is built with the same privacy-first principles as every Team Antigravity app.

The short version: SOTA does not collect personal data, does not require an account, does not use analytics or tracking, and does not connect to a cloud service. The design system is delivered as a local code package and documentation.

1 Data we collect

None. SOTA is a static design system — a collection of tokens, components, CSS, JavaScript, and documentation. There is no sign-up, no telemetry, no analytics, no advertising, and no back-end service operated by Team Antigravity.

2 How you use SOTA

SOTA is distributed as source code and documentation. You run it on your own infrastructure, your own CI/CD, and your own hosting. Any data you choose to collect through the websites or apps you build with SOTA is your own responsibility and governed by your own privacy policy.

3 No accounts, no servers, no tracking

Team Antigravity does not operate a SaaS for SOTA. There is no dashboard, no user account database, no tracking pixels, and no cookies used for analytics or advertising. If you visit the SOTA documentation website, no personal information is collected.

4 Third-party services

SOTA itself does not use third-party services. Websites you build with SOTA may choose their own hosting, analytics, or CMS providers. Those choices are yours and should be documented in your own privacy policy.

5 Open source and transparency

SOTA is released under an open-source license. You can inspect the full source code, tokens, and components at any time. Nothing is hidden, obfuscated, or locked behind a service.

6 Children

SOTA is a general-audience design system and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone because we do not collect personal information at all.

7 Changes & contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the date below. Questions or concerns? Email hello@teamantigravity.com.
