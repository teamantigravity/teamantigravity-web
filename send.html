01 · Peer-to-peer

Files stream directly between your devices

No cloud relay, no third-party server in the middle. Gravity Send discovers nearby devices on your local network and sends files over a direct peer-to-peer connection — as fast as your Wi-Fi allows.

02 · No size limits

Move gigabytes without breaking a sweat

There's no 2 GB upload cap or "premium tier" to unlock larger transfers. Send a 50 GB video project or a single screenshot — the same direct connection handles both.

03 · Cross-platform

Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS — all talking

One codebase, every major platform. Transfer from your Android phone to your Linux desktop or your iPhone to your Windows laptop — they all discover each other automatically.

Why we built it this way

Three rules we don't break.

Direct is faster

Why upload to a server and download again when your devices are on the same network? Peer-to-peer means full-speed transfers with zero detours.

Your files stay yours

No cloud copy is ever created. Once the transfer completes, nothing lingers on any server — because no server was involved in the first place.

No accounts, no friction

Open the app, pick a file, pick a device. That's it. No email, no password, no "sign in with Google" — just a tool that gets out of your way.
