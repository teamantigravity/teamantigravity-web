Gravity Torrent is a next-generation torrent client that lets you stream media while it downloads — on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS, all from one codebase.

Get Gravity Torrent See how it works
01 · Instant streaming

Play media while it downloads

Don't wait for the full download. Gravity Torrent prioritizes sequential pieces so you can start watching or listening within seconds of adding a torrent — like WebTorrent, but on mobile too.

02 · Truly cross-platform

Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS from one codebase

One app, every platform. The same beautiful interface and feature set whether you're on your phone, your laptop, or your desktop. No "lite" mobile version — the full experience everywhere.

03 · Per-torrent controls

Sequential mode, speed limits, tags and deep links

Every torrent gets its own speed limits, sequential mode toggle, notification preferences, and tag. Use deep links to open specific torrents from other apps or the browser.
